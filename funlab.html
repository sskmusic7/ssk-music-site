Fun Lab

Tools built in the studio, opened up so you can mess with them. Everything here runs in your browser — nothing to install.

Stem Replicator

Sixteen bars of a real session, split into its parts. Mute and solo to hear how the record is stacked up — everything stays in sync because the stems never stop, they just get turned down.

Beat Machine

Sixteen steps, seven sounds — every one sliced out of an SSK session. Tap the grid, or send a pattern up from the cheat sheet below.

Drum Pattern Cheat Sheet

Ten classic grooves, one bar each. Hit Preview to hear it, or read it off the grid.

Rhyme Finder

Paste a whole bar — it rhymes off the last word and groups by syllable count so you can match the length.
